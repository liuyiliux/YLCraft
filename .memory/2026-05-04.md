- `GET /live2d/{model_id}/rigging/state` - 获取绑骨状态
- `PUT /live2d/{model_id}/rigging/expression` - 更新表情
- `PUT /live2d/{model_id}/rigging/eye-tracking` - 更新视线跟踪
- `POST /live2d/{model_id}/motion` - 生成待机动作
